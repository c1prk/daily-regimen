Face & posture — what actually moves the needle

Checked against current evidence, not internet trend claims.

01Body fat is the main driver of jaw and cheekbone definition. Facial fat is one of the last places to clear — this follows the same slow timeline as the rest of the recomp.
02Sodium and water retention cause visible puffiness fast (days, not months) — instant noodles are the main culprit here. Hitting your water target offsets this.
03Posture is the highest-leverage lever you actually have. Forward head posture pushes the chin down and compresses the jaw-neck line — the posture work already built into every session below does double duty here.
04Genetics set the ceiling on jaw width, chin projection, and cheekbone height. No training or diet changes that.
Skip: mewing and jaw-exercise tools have no peer-reviewed evidence of reshaping adult bone structure — orthodontic bodies have said so directly. Heavy jaw-clenching or gum chewing can actually enlarge the masseter and widen the jaw, working against the slim look. Not worth the time.
