How this adapts

The program moves for you. You don't have to be perfect to keep progressing.

—
14-day consistency
Not a streak. One missed day doesn't zero this out — it's a rolling average, because that's closer to how progress actually works.
01Hit the top of the rep range on every set → next session, add the smallest weight step and work back up. Stop each set with 1 to 2 reps left in the tank.
02Miss your target by 30%+, two sessions running → drop the weight back one step. That's programmed, not a failure.
03Weeks 6 and 12 are deloads, whether you feel like you need it or not.
04One missed day changes nothing. The plan just picks up where it left off — no makeup sessions, no guilt tax.
05Every session has a 10-minute floor. Busy day? Do the compressed version. Keeping the pattern alive matters more than the full volume.
06Swim as often as you want, but keep lift-day swims easy and do them after lifting, never before an upper day. Swimming is shoulder-heavy, so if your delts feel fried, the swim gets shorter, not the lift.
07Push isolation lifts close to failure. Lateral raises, curls, pushdowns, pec deck: 0 to 1 reps left on the last set. Big machine presses and pulldowns: 1 to 2 left. Growth tracks how close sets get to failure.
08Own the stretch. Control the bottom of every rep where the muscle is longest (arms overhead on pulldowns, arms low on lateral raises). On the last set of lateral raises, when full reps die, keep going with half reps in the bottom half.

Face & posture — what actually moves the needle

Checked against current evidence, not internet trend claims.

01Body fat is the main driver of jaw and cheekbone definition. Facial fat is one of the last places to clear — it follows the same slow timeline as the rest of your physique.
02Sodium and water retention cause visible puffiness fast (days, not months) — instant noodles are the main culprit here. Hitting your water target offsets this.
03Posture is the highest-leverage lever you actually have. Forward head posture pushes the chin down and compresses the jaw-neck line — the posture work already built into every session below does double duty here.
04Genetics set the ceiling on jaw width, chin projection, and cheekbone height. No training or diet changes that.
Skip: mewing and jaw-exercise tools have no peer-reviewed evidence of reshaping adult bone structure — orthodontic bodies have said so directly. Heavy jaw-clenching or gum chewing can actually enlarge the masseter and widen the jaw, working against the slim look. Not worth the time.
